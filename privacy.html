Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Overview

AIR Blackbox is an open-source EU AI Act compliance scanner for Python AI agents. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we process it, and what safeguards we have in place.

TL;DR: We don't collect any data. No tracking, no analytics, no cookies.

What Data We Collect

AIR Blackbox does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data about you or your code.

Scanner (pip install air-blackbox)

The open-source scanner runs entirely on your machine. Your code is never sent to our servers:

  • No telemetry is sent
  • No diagnostics are collected
  • No usage data is tracked
  • Your code stays on your machine

Browser Scanner (airblackbox.ai/scan)

The browser-based scanner runs in your browser using WebAssembly (WASM). Your code is analyzed locally:

  • No code is uploaded to our servers
  • No tracking pixels or analytics
  • No cookies are set
  • Analysis happens entirely in your browser

What We Don't Do

  • We don't use cookies
  • We don't use third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Segment, etc.)
  • We don't embed tracking pixels
  • We don't use advertising networks
  • We don't sell or share your data
  • We don't require an account to use the scanner
